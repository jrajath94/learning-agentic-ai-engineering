Why usage can become large

Each agent call can include prior context, tool results, and new model output. Parallel agents and repeated tests create more calls. Cached input can have different accounting from uncached input. A long conversation does not by itself reveal the billed amount. [U08]

OpenAI reports concurrent agent sessions, including downstream subagents. Its report also states that model prices, activity, and harness behavior affect API-equivalent spend. By mid-October, over two thirds of included researchers averaged a daily peak of at least four concurrent agents. Over 10% averaged a daily peak of at least sixteen. These are peak concurrency figures, not daily totals. Its usage data cover a research organization and may omit some activity. These patterns explain a mechanism. They do not prove the token rate of an individual worker. [U01] [U02]

Measure one task

  1. Choose one local task and record its start and end times.
  2. Record the model, interface, version, and number of calls.
  3. Record input, cached input, output, and reasoning tokens when the provider exposes them.
  4. Record whether cached tokens are a subset of input tokens.
  5. Record retries and subagent calls without double counting.
  6. Record actual billed cost if the account exposes task-level billing.
  7. Otherwise estimate cost from the applicable published rates and label it an estimate.
  8. Check the result against tests and record human review time.

Illustrative calculation: 100,000 uncached input tokens at $2 per million plus 10,000 output tokens at $8 per million equals $0.28. These rates and counts are examples, not current prices or observed usage. If 60,000 input tokens are cached, do not add those tokens again. Apply the applicable cached rate to that subset and the ordinary rate to the remainder. Check the provider’s current terms.

A subscription limit, API list-price equivalent, internal infrastructure cost, and paid invoice can differ. Do not divide a company total by a guessed headcount. Do not infer quality or research progress from usage alone.

Practice and evidence limits

Complete: Add the missing input and output counts to a local task record. Independent: Compare two attempts on the same task and acceptance test. Retrieve: Why is the 90th percentile not the median? Transfer: Account for a cached document in a research task. Delayed review: Recompute the estimate tomorrow from the saved usage record.

What do the agents actually do all day?

OpenAI interviewed ten researchers about their workflows. One described porting an existing evaluation and running ablations to decide if it was worth using. The report estimates much higher autonomous success for monitoring training than for implementing inference or serving code. That difference is a reason to assign each task its own acceptance test.
