Tokens

The 5 to 10 thousand tokens a day figure is not in the sources.

What is on tape: Boris Cherny, May 2025 Latent Space mirror, said internal use was free and a few automated workloads cost thousands of dollars a day. Cat Wu, same episode, said external active users were around $6 a day. A YouTube page for a later talk, no captions, has an unlabeled line about five local agents and hundreds or thousands most nights. Treat that page as medium confidence.

Sequence and state

1. Mechanism

A subscription hides the meter until you hit a cap. The cap is not a token budget you can quote. C97: Anthropic surveyed 132 engineers and researchers in August 2025, interviewed 53, and analyzed 200,000 Claude Code transcripts. The write-up, dated 2 Dec 2025, said Claude was in 59 percent of work and self-reported productivity was 50 percent higher (C98), merged pull requests per engineer per day were 67 percent higher (C99), and more than half could fully delegate only 0 to 20 percent of their work (C102). That study does not publish a daily token allowance.

C38, Meta's pricing page: standard Spark is $1.25 per million input and $4.25 per million output. Contributor mode is $0.10 and $0.20 because training is allowed. The cheaper number is a data term, not a discount to chase on bank code.

2. Artifact

There is no token config to copy. The artifact that limits spend is a short task: tdd-loop.md asks for one test, not a tour of the repo. overnight.md names a stop condition so a long run has a command that can fail it.

3. Worked example

sh kit/scripts/tdd-demo.sh makes one assertion, fails, then passes. It does not open a model. That is the cheapest loop you can rehearse. A model session should look like those two runs plus a diff, not like a restatement of the repository.

4. Failure modes

  • Quoting 5 to 10 thousand tokens a day in a design doc. It is not sourced here.
  • Reading the YouTube "thousands most nights" line as Anthropic's published quota.
  • Turning Contributor mode on to hit the $0.10 price on a private repo.

5. Exercise

For one day, write down each model call next to kit/prompts/tdd-loop.md: tool, task, whether a test ran, whether you kept the diff. Do not invent a token count. Self-check: every kept diff has a command next to it.

6. Your stack

The about $100 Codex seat and the about $20 Claude seat are the two meters you can feel. When one caps, the note in kit/podcasts/karpathy-eval-loop.md is to switch, not to keep a session that can no longer call tools. Do not put a production corpus in the cheaper Muse tier.
