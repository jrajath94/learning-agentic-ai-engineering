Tokens

The 5 to 10 thousand tokens a day figure is not in the sources.

What is on tape: Boris Cherny, May 2025 Latent Space mirror, said internal use was free and a few automated workloads cost thousands of dollars a day. Cat Wu, same episode, said external active users were around $6 a day. A YouTube page for a later talk, no captions, has an unlabeled line about five local agents and hundreds or thousands most nights. Treat that page as medium confidence.

See the shape first

1. Mechanism

A subscription hides the meter until you hit a cap. The cap is not a token budget you can quote. Anthropic's Aug 2025 study, 132 people surveyed, 53 interviews, 200,000 transcripts, said Claude was in 59 percent of work, self-reported productivity was about 50 percent higher, and merged pull requests per engineer per day were 67 percent higher. Most people fully delegated only 0 to 20 percent of tasks. That study does not publish a daily token allowance.

Muse publishes prices. Standard Spark is $1.25 per million input and $4.25 per million output. Contributor mode is $0.10 and $0.20 because training is allowed. The cheaper number is a data term, not a discount to chase on bank code.

2. Artifact

There is no token config to copy. The artifact that limits spend is a short task: tdd-loop.md asks for one test, not a tour of the repo. overnight.md names a stop condition so a long run has a command that can fail it.

3. Worked example

The local demo makes one assertion, fails, then passes. It does not open a model. That is the cheapest loop you can rehearse. A model session should look like those two runs plus a diff, not like a restatement of the repository.

4. Failure modes

  • Quoting 5 to 10 thousand tokens a day in a design doc. It is not sourced here.
  • Reading the YouTube "thousands most nights" line as Anthropic's published quota.
  • Turning Contributor mode on to hit the $0.10 price on a private repo.

5. Exercise

For one day, write down each model call as: tool, task, whether a test ran, whether you kept the diff. Do not invent a token count. Self-check: every kept diff has a command next to it.

6. Your stack

The about $100 Codex seat and the about $20 Claude seat are the two meters you can feel. When one caps, Karpathy's note is to switch, not to keep a session that can no longer call tools. Do not put a production corpus in the cheaper Muse tier.
