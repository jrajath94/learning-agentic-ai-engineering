# Research note with a source on every sentence

Provenance: original synthesis. The hub's own rule: a sentence without a URL does not go in the ledger.

```text
Question: {{QUESTION}}
Pages already fetched: {{URLS}}

For each claim, give the URL, the author, the date on the page, and a quote under 30 words.
If the page does not say it, write "not on this page".
Do not add a source you did not open.
Separate disagreements instead of averaging them.
```
